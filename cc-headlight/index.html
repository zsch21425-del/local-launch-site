Good Questions

Before you ask

How long does a restoration last?
With our UV-cure clear coat, expect years — not the few months a DIY kit gives you. That's why the standard job carries a 3-year written warranty. If a lens hazes inside the window, we come back and re-do it free.
Can every headlight be restored?
Almost all standard polycarbonate lenses, yes — which covers the vast majority of vehicles on the road. What can't be fixed from the outside: moisture inside the housing, cracked lenses, or failed internal reflectors. If we see any of that, we'll tell you before we start and won't charge you for a result we can't stand behind.
How long does it take, and do I need to do anything?
About 45–60 minutes per vehicle. We bring everything we need and walk you around the vehicle when we're done.
My car is only a few years old — do I really need this?
Vehicles as new as 3–5 years old show oxidation if they park outside, and in the South Carolina sun it comes on fast. Condition matters more than age — if the lenses look even slightly cloudy, your night visibility is already down. A quick photo text gets you an honest answer either way.
