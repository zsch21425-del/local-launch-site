78% Hang Up After a Missed Call. Most Trade Shops Still Lose Them During the Day

Local Launch · Sep 2026 · 4 min read

Direct answer: most people will not wait for you to call back. Beside (Jul 9, 2026) cites a CallRail 2025 survey of 1,000 U.S. consumers. 78% have abandoned a business after an unanswered call. Only 42% leave a voicemail. 21% immediately call another business.

RevSquared (Jul 27, 2026) puts the daytime miss even higher. More than 60% of calls to small service businesses go unanswered during business hours. 85% of those missed callers never call back.

That is not only an after-hours problem. That is a Tuesday-at-10am problem. A Greenville HVAC tech on a crawl space. A Simpsonville plumber under a sink. A Mauldin landscaper on a mower.

What actually happens when the phone rings

Someone searches "AC repair near me" or "emergency plumber Greer." They tap the number. You are on a job. The call rings out or hits voicemail.

Most people do not leave a message. About one in five dials the next shop right away. You never hear about the job. Your competitor books it while you finish yours.

Shops undercount the daytime miss. They assume the caller will leave a voicemail. The survey numbers say most will not.

What the market charges to catch those calls

RevSquared puts full-service AI receptionist stacks at $200–$550/mo all-in for most small service businesses. That is still far cheaper than a full-time front desk at about $3,500–$5,000/mo (same guide: about 88–94% less).

Stakd (Apr 23, 2026) puts solo operators under ~50 calls a month in the $99–$199/mo band. Done-for-you at 50–500 calls sits $299–$699. Beside lists a flat $29.99/mo DIY path. Hybrid human+AI desks run higher.

RevSquared's ROI note (Aug 4, 2026) says recovering 10–20% more inbound often covers a year of platform cost in month one. Their plumber example recovered 14 jobs at about $450 each.

Site chat is cheaper still. SchedulingKit (Feb 27, 2026) puts chatbots about $0–$200/mo and voice agents $50–$500/mo. Partoo (Jul 16, 2026) reports Google reintroduced GBP Messaging with a native AI agent after pulling messaging in 2024. Listings that stay answerable matter more when Google's own agent is in the loop.

What Greenville trades should fix this month

  1. Sticky click-to-call on mobile — one number. No hunting.
  2. A short quote form — name, phone, service, city. Not twelve fields.
  3. A pickup when you cannot answer — AI voice, after-hours booking, or a live answer service. Pick one.
  4. Text fallback — missed-call text-back catches people who will not leave voicemail.
  5. Honest GBP hours and phone — wrong hours train people to bounce.
  6. Watch GBP Messaging — if Google's agent routes profile messages, stale listings lose twice.

You do not need a call center. You need the call that hits while you are under a house in Fountain Inn to still become a job.
