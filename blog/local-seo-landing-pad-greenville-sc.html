What a "landing pad" means in Greer or Fountain Inn

Skip the brochure language. A landing pad is a page a homeowner can use in under a minute.

  1. Name the services in plain English. Lawn care, drain clearing, water heater swap — not "full outdoor solutions."
  2. Name the towns. Greer, Travelers Rest, Mauldin, Fountain Inn, Simpsonville. Write them like a person talks.
  3. One-tap Call and Quote on a phone. Sticky if you can. No scavenger hunt.
  4. Proof a stranger can check. Reviews on the page or a clear link out, hours, service area.
  5. Same name, phone, cities, and hours as the Google listing. When the site and the profile disagree, AI answers get messy.

That is the site half. It matches what the service-business local SEO guides keep circling without needing a 40-page content farm.

The living-listing half (this is where most shops stall)

A clean site with a quiet Google profile still looks half-dead.

After the build:

  • Post on the Google Business Profile once a week. One real photo. One line about a real job.
  • Ask for a review after finished work. Same day when you can. Ask them to name the job and the town.
  • Reply to reviews. The good ones and the hard ones. Short and specific.
  • Keep categories, hours, and service area honest. Google's own local ranking tips still start with a complete, accurate profile.

That is review velocity in plain English: new proof keeps showing up. Not a 2022 pile of five-stars.

Local note: a Mauldin HVAC truck or Fountain Inn painter does not need three marketing PDFs. They need service pages that match the towns they cover, plus a Google listing that still moves every week.

How GEO fits without a science project

GEO sounds fancy. For a three-truck shop it mostly means:

  • Service pages AI can quote
  • FAQ answers people actually ask ("Do you cover Pelzer?" "What does a standard clean cost?")
  • A Google listing that still looks alive
  • Name, phone, and cities that match everywhere

You do not need a national "AI SEO" retainer to start. You need pages and a profile that tell the truth.
