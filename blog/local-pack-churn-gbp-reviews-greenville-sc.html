41% of Local Pack Leaders Fell Off. Weekly GBP Posts Still Win in Greenville

Local Launch · Sep 2026 · 4 min read

Direct answer: more than 30% of local Google queries now show AI Overviews. Across 2,000+ US profiles, 41% of the 2024 Local Pack top-3 shops were gone by mid-2026. Listings that still get cited usually share three things: a keyword-rich Google Business Profile description, reviews that name the actual service, and real location pages. Miss one and you are about 3.2× less likely to show up. Weekly GBP posts put you in the Local Pack about 3.1× more often than monthly-or-less posting. For a Greenville plumber or landscaper, that means the boring weekly update beats a pile of generic five-star reviews.

Most Upstate owners still treat the Google profile like a static business card. That is the problem.

What flipped in local search

Flento (updated August 2026) tracked Local Pack churn and AI Overview citations. The pack is not stable. Almost half of yesterday's top three are gone.

Zero-click is the default now. Flento puts zero-click above 65% of US Google searches. Your Google Business Profile is often the page people actually see — not your website homepage.

That is why a Mauldin HVAC tech can "have a site" and still lose the job to whoever Google names in the box.

Reviews: name the service, not just the vibe

Star count alone is a weak signal for AI answers. Flento's same dataset found reviews that name the service are 2.3× more likely to land in AI Overview citations than higher-count generic five-stars.

"Great guys!" does almost nothing.
"Fixed our AC in Simpsonville same day" gives Google words it can reuse.

When you ask for a review, prompt the real job: city + service. Your Care stack should do that every week, not once a year.

Weekly posts beat monthly silence

Profiles that post on Google Business Profile at least weekly sit in the Local Pack 3.1× more often than shops that post monthly or less. Flento and 12AM Agency both flag that gap — and it widened about 22% from late 2025 into mid-2026.

Weekly does not mean fluff. One photo of a Fountain Inn driveway wash. One line about fall gutter cleanouts in Greer. One offer with a real end date. That is enough.

GEO is just "get named"

Orkkid, Capture That Media, and RankOps frame GEO the same way: structure your facts so ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews name your business.

Tactics that show up in that research stack:

  • Statistics with inline sources (up to about +40% AI visibility in the Princeton-linked tests they cite)
  • Expert quotes (~+41%) and plain stats (~+30%)
  • First citations often land in 4–8 weeks on stronger domains; newer domains can take 3–6 months
