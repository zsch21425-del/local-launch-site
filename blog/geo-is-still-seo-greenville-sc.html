What "GEO is still SEO" looks like on an Upstate trade site

Skip the brochure words. For Fountain Inn pressure washing or Travelers Rest landscaping, citation-ready still looks like this:

  1. Name, phone, hours, and service area match on the site and Google listing
  2. One clear page per main service, with real towns named (Greer, Mauldin, Simpsonville — not vague "Upstate coverage")
  3. LocalBusiness + FAQ schema that matches what you actually do
  4. Short, extractable answers — what you fix, where you work, how to book
  5. A living listing — weekly Google posts, fresh photos, review asks after finished jobs
  6. Same-day reply when Maps or the form pings you

None of that is a secret AI ritual. It is local SEO done clean enough that ChatGPT or Google AI Overviews can name you without guessing.
