2026 Website Cost Guides Still Quote $1,500–$35,000. Greenville Shops Can Start at a Fixed $599

Local Launch · Oct 2026 · 4 min read

Direct answer: 2026 cost guides still put most professional small-business sites in the multi-thousand range. Surmado’s 2026 cost guide lists freelancers at $1,500–$8,000, agencies at $6,000–$35,000+, and says most professional builds land around $3,000–$10,000. BK Web Designs (updated April 2026) puts professional work at $1,500–$15,000. Mainsail’s 50-build write-up stacks tiers from DIY under $500 up past $20,000.

A Mauldin HVAC owner or Greer painter who Googles “website cost” still lands those big bands. Waiting for a four-figure budget while the shop has no site, or a ©2016 footer, is how calls walk next door.

What the 2026 cost pages actually say

We re-read three public cost guides on October 1, 2026. No invented conversion rates. Just their published bands.

Surmado (anchor): DIY $17–$50/mo. Freelancer $1,500–$8,000. Agency $6,000–$35,000+. Managed $99–$500/mo. Most pro builds $3,000–$10,000.

Same story elsewhere: Mainsail’s tiers run DIY under $500, cheap freelancer $500–$2,500, lean studio $2,500–$8,000, mid agency $8,000–$20,000, full agency $20,000+. BK lists freelancers $1,500–$8,000, boutique shops $3,500–$12,000, and full-service agencies $10,000–$35,000+.

Those ranges are real. They are also easy to misread. A Fountain Inn plumber does not need a $12,000 brand system to get the phone ringing.

What a Greenville crew is actually buying

For a Simpsonville pressure-washing crew, the job is simple:

  1. A real site with the same name, phone, and hours as Google
  2. A call button that stays on the phone
  3. Clear service pages that name real towns like Greer and Mauldin. Not vague “Upstate coverage.”
  4. A short form that hits a phone someone answers the same day
  5. A living Google listing with weekly posts, job photos, and review asks after finished work

That is call-and-trust work. Not a portfolio site.

Guides aimed at service businesses keep saying the same boring list: call paths, proof, local trust. Examples we logged September 30, 2026: Impact Marketing on what converts for service businesses and Brandignity on service-based design trends. Cost pages scare owners into waiting. Waiting is the expensive part.
