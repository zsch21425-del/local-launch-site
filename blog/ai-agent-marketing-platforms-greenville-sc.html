What a Greenville crew should buy first

Skip the brochure words. For Fountain Inn pressure washing or Travelers Rest landscaping, the order is boring on purpose:

  1. A real site with name, phone, hours, and service area that match Google
  2. One clear page per main service, with real towns named (Greer, Mauldin, Simpsonville — not vague "Upstate coverage")
  3. LocalBusiness + FAQ schema that matches what you actually do
  4. Sticky click-to-call and a short form that hits a phone someone answers
  5. A living listing — weekly Google posts, fresh photos, review asks after finished jobs
  6. Same-day reply when Maps or the form pings you
  7. Then chat or an AI receptionist — only after the funnel works

None of that requires a multi-agent retainer on day one. It is the work those platforms assume you already finished.
