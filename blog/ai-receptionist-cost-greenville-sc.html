What Does an AI Receptionist Cost in Greenville, SC in 2026?

Local Launch · Aug 2026 · 3 min read

Direct answer: an AI receptionist for a Greenville, SC small business costs about $99 to set up and $19 a month to run with Local Launch. It answers calls and texts 24/7 and hands off to a real person when the conversation needs one.

That price is our own, not a national average. And it's not a niche add-on anymore. SitesByNick's April 2026 checklist for small-business websites lists AI chat as standard equipment. The bot answers around the clock and qualifies leads. For a plumber or landscaper with the phone in the truck, that's the difference between a booked week and a missed call.

What you actually get

  • Calls answered after hours. The 6pm caller who needs a water heater tomorrow doesn't scroll to the next name.
  • Real answers, not voicemail. The AI knows your services, your service area, and your hours.
  • Leads in writing. Booking requests and contact info come to you as a message, not a voicemail you find at 9pm.
  • A handoff when it matters. Big jobs and upset customers get routed back to you.

What it costs

$99 to set up. $19 a month after that. That's $228 a year.

To put it in Greenville terms: a single plumber's service call runs $75 to $150. Miss one call a month and it's paid for. Many shops miss more than that.

Who it's for

Solo operators and small crews. Plumbers, landscapers, HVAC, painters, junk removal. The trades where the owner is the one answering. If you've missed a call because you were under a sink or on a mower, this is aimed at you.

Where it falls short

Be honest about it: AI is not a person. It should not quote a $12,000 roof from a two-minute call. It should capture the lead, answer the basics, and get the customer to you.

It's also not a replacement for a good website or a good Google Business Profile. Those get you found. The receptionist catches the calls after that.

Set it up right

Give it your real services and real hours. Tell it your service area, not "we serve everywhere." Then test it with a friend's call and fix what sounds robotic. A receptionist you don't trust is one you won't use.

The Greenville note

Greenville customers call after work. That's when most small shops are off the clock. An AI receptionist answers when you can't.
