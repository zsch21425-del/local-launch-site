What a Greenville crew should ship first

For Travelers Rest landscaping or Simpsonville pressure washing, keep the order boring:

  1. A real site with the same name, phone, and hours as Google
  2. A call button that stays on the phone screen
  3. One clear page per main service, with real towns named (Greer, Mauldin, Easley — not vague “Upstate coverage”)
  4. A short form that hits a phone someone answers the same day
  5. Owner or crew proof — real photos, real reviews answered
  6. A living Google listing — weekly posts, fresh job photos, review asks after finished jobs
  7. Then fancy motion, multi-page brand systems, or big agency retainers

None of that requires matching a design shop’s 100+ review pile on day one.
