What that means for a Greenville-area trade

If you run pressure washing in Simpsonville or landscaping out of Travelers Rest, you do not need a separate Gemini project. You need a living listing that matches the site.

Do this weekly:

  1. One real Google post. Finished job, seasonal offer, or hours update. Not a stock quote graphic.
  2. Fresh photos from the truck or job site when you have them. Twice a month is enough to start.
  3. Review asks after finished work. Text or email the same day while the customer still remembers you.
  4. Name, phone, hours, and service area match the website. A wrong number on Maps kills the call.
  5. Same-day reply when Maps or the form pings you.

That is Care in plain English. Weekly posts plus review cadence. Not another dashboard.
