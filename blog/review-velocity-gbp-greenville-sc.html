What "review velocity" means in plain English

Velocity is not your total star count.

It is whether new reviews keep showing up week after week.

A Fountain Inn painter with 28 reviews, three of them from this month, often looks more alive than a competitor with 180 reviews that stopped in 2024. Customers notice. Google Business Profile activity looks fresher. AI answer engines that pull local names also prefer businesses that still look open and busy.

What to do after every job in Greer or Travelers Rest

Keep it boring and repeatable:

  1. Finish the job. Leave the place clean.
  2. Text or email a short review link the same day. One sentence: "If we did right by you, a Google review helps neighbors find us."
  3. Ask them to name the work and the town. "Replaced the water heater in Simpsonville" beats "Great guys!"
  4. Reply to every review within a few days. Yes, the hard ones too. Calm and specific.
  5. Post on the Google Business Profile weekly. One real photo. One line about a real job. That is enough.

Do not pay for fake reviews. Do not run a gift-card scheme that breaks Google's rules. Steady real asks win.

Local note: a one-time review blitz years ago looks frozen on a Greenville, Mauldin, or Fountain Inn listing. Weekly asks and weekly Google posts look like a shop that is still working.
