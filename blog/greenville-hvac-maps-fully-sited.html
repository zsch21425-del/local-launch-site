What that means if you run HVAC here

If you already have a domain, you are not behind on "getting online." You are in the same boat as every shop Google already shows.

The fight moved:

  1. Who loads fast on a phone — Impact Marketing Solutions (Jul 8, 2026), citing eDesign Interactive's look at 200+ plumber/HVAC/electrician/roofer sites, puts conversion near 32% under 2 seconds, 18% at 3–5 seconds, and 6% past 5 seconds.
  2. Who keeps the quote form short — same report: single-field "Get a quote" near ~12%, three fields ~4%, five-plus fields 1–2%.
  3. Who gets found when AI names 1–2 shops — ranking somewhere on page one is not the short list.
  4. Who posts and answers on Google every week — stale profiles lose to shops that stay active.

PERC Engage (Mar 9, 2026) still puts about 60% of local service searches on mobile. Click-to-call lifts conversion about ~28%. Mobile forms with four fields or fewer convert about 160% better. 53% leave after a load longer than 3 seconds.

A Greer tech with a slow five-page brochure site loses the same lead a fast competitor keeps. A Mauldin shop with no sticky call button loses the homeowner who will not scroll.

What Greenville HVAC should fix this month

  1. Speed first — under 2 seconds on mobile. That is the conversion cliff in the trade-site data.
  2. Sticky click-to-call — one number. No hunting.
  3. Short quote form — name, phone, service, city. Prefer one field when you can.
  4. Service pages that answer real searches — "AC repair Greenville," "heat pump install Greer," not a vague About blurb.
  5. Weekly Google Business posts + review replies — the ranked shops already have reviews. Fresh activity is how you stay visible.
  6. Honest hours and service area — wrong hours train people to bounce to the next Maps pin.

If you still have no site in a smaller town or a long-tail niche, build one. On Greenville HVAC page one, assume your peers already did.
