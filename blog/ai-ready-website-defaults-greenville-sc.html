What "AI-ready" actually means on a Greenville service site

Skip the buzzwords. On a real site it looks like this:

  1. LocalBusiness (and service) schema that matches the name, phone, cities, and hours on your Google listing.
  2. FAQ schema on pages people actually ask about — cost ranges, service area, emergency vs booked work.
  3. One clean page per main service, with the towns you cover named in plain English (Greer, Travelers Rest, Fountain Inn — not "Greater Upstate metro").
  4. A fast page that answers the phone or form the same day. A fancy chatbot on a dead lead form still loses the job.
  5. A Google Business Profile that gets weekly posts and steady review asks. AI answer engines pull living listings more often than frozen ones.

That is the boring half of every 2026 "AI features" guide. The flashy half — personalization engines, auto-generated blog farms, six chat products stacked on one homepage — is optional for a three-truck crew.
