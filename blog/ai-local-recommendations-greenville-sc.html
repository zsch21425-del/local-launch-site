Questions

How do I check whether AI recommends my business?

Open ChatGPT and Google and ask for the service you provide in your town — "best plumber in Greenville", "who does mulch in Simpsonville". If your name does not come up, that is your starting point. Do it again in a month to see whether anything moved.

Which sources do AI answers actually pull from?

Mostly sources it already trusts: a complete and active Google Business Profile, recent reviews, and pages it can read cleanly with a consistent name, address and phone. There is no trick to it — the same signals that rank you locally are the ones that get you named.

Does a website matter, or is the Google profile enough?

Both. The profile gets you into the conversation; the site is where the customer decides. AI answers routinely cite the business's own pages, so a site with clear service pages and schema is what lets you be quoted rather than just listed.

How long does it take to show up in AI answers?

Faster than traditional ranking, but not overnight. Profile corrections and schema can be picked up within days; broader visibility builds as reviews and content accumulate. It compounds in the same way SEO does.
