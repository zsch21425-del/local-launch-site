The order most Greenville shops actually need

A Fountain Inn plumber on a job cannot babysit five dashboards. The sequence that holds up in the field is boring and fixed:

  1. A site that loads, states the trade, and takes a lead. Phone, form, or booking link above the fold. Cities you actually cover.
  2. A complete Google Business Profile. Categories, hours, service area, real photos, weekly posts.
  3. Review velocity with service + city named. “Fixed our AC in Greer” beats “great guy.”
  4. Answer speed. Form and call response measured in minutes, not days.
  5. Then an AI receptionist or chat layer if you still miss calls while you are under a sink.

Skip step 1 and bolt on step 5, and the bot answers questions your site never proved. Skip the profile and the AI marketing manager has nothing clean to cite.

Quick check: if your site cannot take a lead on mobile in under 30 seconds, fix that before you pay for another AI front desk.

What that means before you buy another tool

If your site is missing or stuck on a 2016 template, fix the site. An AI receptionist will not rank you in the Greenville Maps pack by itself.

If the site is solid and modern, the leak is often findability and follow-up — weekly GBP work, review asks, GEO so AI answers can name you — not another full “AI marketing manager.”

If calls already go to voicemail while you are on roofs in Travelers Rest, then a front-desk AI is a real miss-call fix. Add it after the site path is real. Not as the first spend.
