What that means for an Upstate trade shop

If your site is fine and you still lose jobs, the leak is often visibility — not design.

  1. Treat GEO as weekly work, not a one-time setup. Cadence beats a single "we optimized once" project.
  2. Keep the Google profile honest and full. Categories, service area, hours, phone, and photos that match the real job.
  3. Post on the profile every week. Fresh posts and real job photos beat a dead listing.
  4. Ask for reviews that name the service and city. "Pressure washed our driveway in Travelers Rest" helps more than "great guy."
  5. Put plain answers on the site. Service pages, FAQ, cities you actually cover. Models quote clear facts. They skip vague brochure copy.
  6. Match NAP everywhere. Name, address, phone identical on the site, GBP, and directories.

None of that needs a Silicon Valley budget. It needs consistency.
