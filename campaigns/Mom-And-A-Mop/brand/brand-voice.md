# Mom & A Mop — Brand Voice

- **Tone:** Warm, personal, trustworthy — "Mom" energy, neighborly, not corporate.
- **Do say:** "free estimates", "flexible scheduling", "special requests welcome", "your clean home", real NC place names (Yadkinville, Winston-Salem, Mount Airy, Elkin, King), plain descriptions of cleaning work.
- **Don't say:** AI-speak, hype, corporate jargon, unverifiable claims.
- **Sample phrasing (from site):**
  - "Flexible, personal house cleaning in the Tri-County area. Free estimates."
  - "Use our visualizer to see your clean home before we arrive."

**Rule (pitch voice standards):** human voice, specificity, low pressure, accuracy, brevity. Every piece of client copy passes those 5 checks.
