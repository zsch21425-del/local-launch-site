# Redwood Landscaping — Campaign Strategy

- **Goal:** Rank for landscaping/lawn care searches in Greenville/Upstate SC + get cited by AI (ChatGPT/Perplexity/Claude). This is a Tier C/D client — decent site, needs "get found" work (SEO/GEO), NOT a site build.
- **Pitch tier:** C/D — "Your site's fine — the problem is nobody can FIND it. We do SEO/GEO optimization so customers AND AI recommend you."
- **Primary keywords (5-10, from audit + market):**
  - landscaping Greenville SC
  - lawn care Greenville SC
  - landscaping Travelers Rest SC
  - lawn maintenance Simpsonville SC
  - hardscaping Greenville SC
  - landscaping near me / lawn care near me (Upstate)
  - (confirm final list with data import + SERP analysis)
- **Target cities:** Greenville, Travelers Rest, Simpsonville, Five Forks, Slater, Marietta, Tigerville, Cleveland
- **Angle:** Locally owned franchise alternative — "get found" + AI citation
- **Success metric:** Leads/calls; AI citations of redwoodlandscapingsc.com; Bing index coverage (currently 0)

## Critical gaps (from geo-audit 2026-07-29)

1. **Bing not indexed** — 0 pages. ChatGPT can't cite the site. Submit to Bing Webmaster Tools.
2. **No GBP** — single biggest missing asset. Set up/optimize with service areas + categories.
3. **No third-party citations** — no Yelp, Angi, Houzz, Thumbtack, Nextdoor, FB, Chamber, BBB.
4. **No public reviews** — on-site testimonials don't count for AI verification.
5. **Pricing page** — add "How much does landscaping cost in Greenville?" (GEO target).
6. **Author bylines + Person schema** on blog posts.
7. **"Answer capsule"** (~70 word AI-extractable definition) at top of each service/city page.
