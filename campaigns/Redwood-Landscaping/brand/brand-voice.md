# Redwood Landscaping — Brand Voice

- **Tone:** Local, trustworthy, plainspoken. Neighbor talking to neighbor. No corporate jargon, no hype.
- **Do say:** "free estimate", "licensed & insured", "locally owned", "the Upstate", real place names (Slater, Marietta, Travelers Rest, Greenville), plain descriptions of work (mowing, mulching, hardscaping).
- **Don't say:** AI-speak, industry jargon without explanation, hype adjectives, "we're the best" claims, anything not verifiable.
- **Sample phrasing (from on-site testimonials + site copy):**
  - "Striped Lawn — Travelers Rest" (gallery caption naming real locations)
  - Testimonial voices: Mike T. (Five Forks), Sarah K. (Slater), David R. (Travelers Rest)

**Rule (from pitch voice standards):** human voice, specificity, low pressure, accuracy, brevity. Every piece of client-facing copy passes those 5 checks.
