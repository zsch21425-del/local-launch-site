- **Why it matters:** Local-service queries on page 1 are dominated by entities with citation mass — Google uses these third-party mentions to confirm the business is real and where it is. Each consistent listing (same NAP) is a vote of trust. Facebook is also where local homeowners in Greenville and the Upstate actually look for and message service providers.
- **Do:** Create each profile with the exact NAP and link back to redwoodlandscapingsc.com. On each, list the same service categories (lawn care, mowing, landscaping, mulch, leaf removal) and the same service-area towns.
