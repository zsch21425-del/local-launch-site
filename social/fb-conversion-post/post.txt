A website has one job for a trade business: get the phone to ring. Not win awards.

Here's what "built to convert" actually looks like in 2026:

• A call button that's always visible — one tap from anywhere on the page.
• Service-specific pages, not one "we do everything" blob.
• Straight answers to the questions people actually type — "how much does mulch cost?", "do you serve Mauldin?"
• Something that proves you're real — reviews, a license, a before/after.

A generic template tells a homeowner you exist. That's about it.

If you're a service business in the Upstate, it's worth a 5-minute check to see what your website is actually doing for you.
