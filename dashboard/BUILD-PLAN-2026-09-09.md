# Build Plan — Local Launch OS (flagship redesign)

Source: Astra (GPT-6) build-plan pass, 2026-09-09. Each page gets a DISTINCT
signature treatment so the whole app reads as a curated showcase, not one
repeated style. Dark canvas + Space Grotesk + Instrument Serif throughout.

1. Prospect detail — "The Project Room" — ARCHITECTURE
   Brutalist courtyard at blue hour. Work nav: Pitch / Demo / Playbook / Activity.
   Prompt: "Brutalist courtyard at blue hour, black basalt walls, narrow cyan-lit
   doorway, shallow reflecting pool, architectural editorial photography,
   panoramic, empty left third, no people or text."

2. Approvals — "The Decision Desk" — TEARING
   Ivory decision sheet w/ one torn edge. Approve / Request changes.
   Prompt: "Macro studio photograph of thick ivory cotton paper torn across matte
   charcoal backing, dramatic side lighting, visible paper fibers, restrained
   editorial still life, panoramic crop, no text."

3. Demos — "The Exhibition" — GALLERY STAGING
   Website previews as collectible objects; one oversized live-preview frame.
   Prompt: "Obsidian exhibition plinth beneath a suspended blank landscape display,
   warm white spotlight, infinite dark gallery, premium industrial product
   photography, no interface or lettering."

4. Reports — "The Observatory" — GLASSMORPHISM
   Three headline metrics in frosted glass panels over an atmospheric horizon.
   Prompt: "Abstract teal atmospheric horizon above a black ocean, diffused luminous
   mist, subtle photographic grain, low contrast center, panoramic, no objects or text."

5. Fleet — "The Ensemble" — SCULPTURAL PORTRAITS
   Agent roster with titanium-kinetic-sculpture portraits.
   Prompt: "Distinct brushed titanium kinetic sculptures on black, cyan rim lighting,
   museum collection photography, isolated forms, no faces or text."

6. Automation — "The Infrastructure" — ARCHITECTURE
   Steel bridge trusses; workflow lanes + run state.
   Prompt: "Night photograph beneath a monumental steel bridge, precise repeating
   trusses, amber maintenance lights, deep black shadows, no text."

7. Clients / Leads — "The Directory" — TYPOGRAPHIC INDEX
   Embossed archival dividers; name-first sortable rows.
   Prompt: "Macro black embossed archival dividers, raking silver light, abstract
   stepped edges, panoramic, no lettering."

Execution order: prospect detail → approvals → demos → reports → fleet →
automation → clients/leads.
