About

Protecting Greenville Homes
for Over 25 Years

Carolina Pest Patrol is a locally owned and operated pest control company serving Greenville, South Carolina and the surrounding Upstate region. Since 1998, we've been helping homeowners and businesses protect their properties from unwanted pests — from ants and roaches to termites, rodents, and wildlife.

We believe in doing the job right the first time. Every treatment plan is tailored to your specific situation, using a combination of proven techniques and the latest pest control technology. Our licensed technicians are trained, background-checked, and committed to providing the highest level of service.
