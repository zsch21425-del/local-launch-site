SMS Consent / Opt-In Disclosure

SMS Communication — Opt-In

When you provide your mobile phone number to Local Launch, you consent to receive text (SMS) messages from Local Launch related to the services you have requested. By opting in, you agree to these terms.

What you'll receive

  • Alerts and notifications about your website, search, and marketing services.
  • Confirmations, appointment and follow-up messages tied to your request.
  • Operational updates that require your attention or approval.

Consent

By providing your number and opting in, you consent to receive automated text messages from Local Launch. Message and data rates may apply. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

How to opt back in

If you previously opted out and wish to resume receiving messages, reply START to the last message you received, or contact us and ask to be re-added.

How to opt out

You can opt out of SMS at any time by replying STOP to any text message you receive from us, or by contacting us directly. After you opt out, you will no longer receive automated text messages, though you may still receive service messages as required.
