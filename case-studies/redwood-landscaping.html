The Problem

Redwood Landscaping had no website, no Google Business Profile optimization, and zero online presence. When potential customers searched for landscaping services in Slater-Marietta, Greenville County, and surrounding areas — Redwood simply did not exist.

Their competition had decade-old websites, basic SEO, and Google Maps listings. Redwood was invisible.

What We Built

🌐

Custom Website

Fast, mobile-first site built on a modern stack. No bloated page builders — clean HTML + Tailwind deployed on Vercel.

🎯

Location Pages

Targeted pages for Slater, Marietta, Greenville County, and service areas — each optimized for local search.

🤖

AI Visualizer

Customers upload a photo of their yard and see an AI-generated transformation — a before/after slider. Powered by Gemini.

📸

Project Gallery

8 real job photos showcasing actual work — striped lawns, mulch beds, landscaping, and backyard transformations.

📝

SEO Blog

2,000+ word blog posts targeting local keywords like "Upstate SC red clay lawn care" — authority content that ranks.

📊

Schema Markup

JSON-LD LocalBusiness schema on every page — telling Google exactly who, where, and what services are offered.

The Stack

Frontend: Vanilla HTML + Tailwind CSS + Vanilla JS

Hosting: Vercel (free tier — zero hosting cost)

AI: Gemini 2.5 Flash Image (yard visualizer)

SEO: Lighthouse + Screaming Frog + manual schema optimization

Deployment: Vercel REST API — automatic deploys from local build
